Named for the stone that sets the line

A cornerstone is the first stone laid in a building. Every other stone is placed in relation to it, so it has to be true. That is how we think about patient safety: get the first things right, every time, and everything built on them stands.

Corner Stone Hospital serves families in Kabba and the surrounding area, with doctors who explain, nurses who listen and a team that treats every patient with respect.

  • Safety firstChecked medicines, clean wards and clear handovers between every shift.
  • Straight answersWe tell you what is wrong, what we recommend and what it will cost, in plain language.
  • Care that stays with youWe follow up after you go home, because recovery does not end at the door.
